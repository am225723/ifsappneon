Pause.

Place one hand near your heart or wherever feels comforting.

Take a gentle breath.

Now notice what is hard right now.

You do not have to explain it.

You do not have to fix it.

Just name it quietly.

This is hard.

Now remind yourself, a part of me is struggling, and that part deserves compassion.

Silently say, I see you.

I am here with you.

We can take this one step at a time.

Take one more breath.

Let the words land as much as they can.

You do not need to feel perfect compassion.

A small pause is enough.
