Welcome to meeting yourself.

This practice is an invitation to connect with the core of you that can bring calm, curiosity, compassion, clarity, courage, confidence, creativity, and connectedness.

Beneath the busy thoughts, the worries, the roles you play in the emotions that come and go, there may be a steadier presence within you.

In IFS, we call this self-energy.

You do not have to make self-energy happen.

You are simply creating space to notice what is already available, even if it is only a small glimmer.

Begin by finding a comfortable position.

You may close your eyes, soften your gaze, or look gently at one steady point.

Let your hands rest.

Let your shoulders soften.

Let your breath be natural.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out, again breathing in and breathing out.

Now begin to notice your body.

Notice your feet, your legs, your hips, your back, your chest, your shoulders, your arms, your hands, your neck, your face.

You do not have to relax everything.

Just notice.

Now bring your attention inside.

Ask gently.

What parts of me are present right now?

You might notice a busy part, a tired part.

a worried part, a part that wants to do this correctly, a part that does not want to do this at all, a part that feels curious, a part that feels guarded.

All parts are welcome to be noticed.

Now ask,

How do I feel toward these parts?

If you feel open, kind, or curious, notice that.

If you feel frustrated, impatient, afraid, blank, or judgmental, notice that too.

That may mean another part is blended.

Nothing is wrong.

If there is a part judging or managing the experience, gently acknowledge it.

You might say inside,

I see you trying to help.

You don't have to go away.

Would you be willing to give me just a little space so I can listen?

Notice what happens.

even a tiny bit of space matters.

Now imagine your inner system like a room.

Or if it feels more natural, imagine yourself as a wide sky.

Thoughts may be clouds passing through.

Emotions may be weather moving across the horizon.

The sky does not have to push anything away.

It simply has space to hold what is here.

Parts may be standing close, some may be far away, some may be hidden, some may be loud, some may be quiet.

And somewhere in this room, there is also the possibility of self-energy, not as a perfect state, not as something you have to perform, just as a steady presence.

Begin with calm.

Notice whether there is even 1% more calm available, maybe in your breath, maybe in your hands, maybe in the space around you.

Now, curiosity.

Can you be just a little curious about what is happening inside without needing to fix it?

Now compassion, is there any part of you that could receive a little kindness?

Even if compassion feels far away, maybe you can notice the wish for compassion.

Now, clarity.

Is there anything you can see a little more clearly about your inner world right now?

Now courage, is there a small brave step in simply being present with yourself?

Now confidence, not forced confidence.

Just the possibility that you can be with what is here, one breath at a time.

Now creativity, maybe your system has more options than it first seemed.

Maybe something new can happen slowly.

Now connectedness.

Notice your connection to your body, to the room, to this moment, and to the parts of you that are trying in their own ways to help.

Now silently ask, what quality of self energy feels most available right now?

and ask what part of me needs that quality most.

If a part comes forward, imagine offering that quality gently.

Not pushing it, not overwhelming it, just letting it be near.

You might say, I am here.

I want to understand.

You do not have to carry this alone.

Notice what happens inside.

Now take a slow breath and let your attention widen.

Feel the support underneath you.

Notice the room around you.

Notice sounds.

Notice light.

Notice your body.

Before you finish, ask yourself, what is one self-energy quality I want to carry into the rest of my day?

When you are ready, gently return.

And as you move back into your day, remember, self-energy does not have to arrive as a grand experience.

Sometimes it begins as one softer breath, one moment of curiosity, one kind word toward a part.

That is enough.

You can return here one breath at a time.
