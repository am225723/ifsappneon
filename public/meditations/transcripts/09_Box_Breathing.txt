Welcome to Box Breathing.

This is a simple breathing practice that can help your system slow down and find a steadier rhythm.

We will breathe in for four counts, hold for four counts, breathe out for four counts, and hold again for four counts.

If holding your breath does not feel good, you can simply breathe gently and skip the holds.

Let this practice be flexible.

Begin by settling into a comfortable position.

Let your shoulders soften if they can.

Let your jaw loosen.

Let your hands rest.

Take one natural breath in and let it out.

Now we'll begin.

Breathe in one, two, three, four, hold one,

two three four breathe out one two three four hold one two

Three, four, good, again, breathe in.

One, two, three, four, hold.

One, two, three, four.

Breathe out.

One, two, three, four.

Hold.

One, two, three, four.

Let your body follow the rhythm as much as it wants to.

Breathe in.

One, two, three, four.

Hold.

On.

Two, three, four.

Breathe out.

One, two, three, four.

Hold.

One, two, three, four.

If your mind wanders, that is okay.

Just come back to the next count.

Breathe in.

One, two, three, four.

Hold.

One, two, three, four.

Breathe out.

One, two, three, four.

Hold.

One, two, three, four.

Now let the counting become softer.

Breathe in for four.

Hold for four.

Breathe out for four.

Hold for four.

Again breathe in.

Hold.

Breathe out.

Hold.

Now release the counting, let your breathing return to whatever pace feels natural.

Notice your body.

Notice your breath.

Notice whether anything inside feels even slightly more settled.

If parts of you still feel activated, that is okay.

The goal is not to force calm.

The goal is to offer your system a steady rhythm.

Take one final breath in and let it out slowly.

When you are ready, return your attention to the room around you.

Let this rhythm be something you can come back to.

Not as a command to calm down, but as a steady square of support your body can lean on when it is ready.
