Welcome to the unburdening ceremony.

This is a gentle visualisation, practice for imagining what it might be like for a part to release something it has carried.

Only do this if the part feels ready.

Unburdening cannot be forced.

If the part is not ready, this practice can simply become a listening practice.

Begin by settling your body.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Bring to mind a part that has been carrying something heavy.

Maybe shame, fear, responsibility, loneliness, guilt, a belief about itself or a belief about the world.

Notice this part gently.

Ask, is it okay for me to be with you right now?

If the answer is no, thank the part for letting you know.

You can stop here or simply offer presence.

If the answer is yes, ask, what burden have you been carrying?

Let the answer come slowly.

Now ask, how long have you been carrying this?

And who or what gave this to you?

Listen without forcing details.

Now ask, do you want to keep carrying this burden or are you interested in imagining what release might feel like?

Respect the answer.

If the part is not ready, say, I will not force you.

We can go slowly.

If the part is ready, imagine a safe place where release can happen.

Maybe near water, fire, earth, wind, light, a sacred container, a field or a tree.

Let the part choose.

Ask the part.

How would you like to release this burden?

It may place it in water, burn it safely in fire, bury it in earth.

Let wind carry it, give it to light, set it down or transform it.

Let the part choose the image.

Now imagine the burden leaving the part only at the pace that feels safe.

No rush, no force, the part remains in control.

As the burden releases, ask, what do you want to receive instead?

Maybe lightness, play, rest, worthiness, protection, love, choice or belonging.

Let the part receive only what feels welcome.

Now notice how the part looks or feels after releasing even a small amount.

Ask, what do you need from me?

So this release can continue safely over time.

Listen.

Offer reassurance I will not abandon you after this.

You do not have to change all at once.

We can keep learning together.

Now thank the part.

Thank any protectors who allowed this process.

Thank your system for moving at its own pace.

Bring attention back to your body.

Feel the support beneath you Take a slow breath in

and let it out.

Return gently to the room.
