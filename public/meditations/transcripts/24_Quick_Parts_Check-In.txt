Take a moment to check in inside and ask, what part of me is most present right now?

Notice the first thing that comes.

It might be a thought, a feeling, a body sensation, or an impulse.

Now ask, what is this part trying to help me with?

Listen gently and now ask, what does this part need for me in the next few minutes?

Maybe space, reassurance, a boundary, rest, a breath, or a small action.

Say to the part, I see that you are here, I want to understand.

You do not have to take over completely.

Take one slow breath and carry this awareness into what comes next.
