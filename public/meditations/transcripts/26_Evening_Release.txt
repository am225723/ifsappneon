Pause at the end of the day.

Let your body know that this day is ending.

Take a slow breath in and let it out.

Notice one thing you are still carrying.

A thought, a feeling, a worry, a conversation.

A part that is still working.

Now say gently, thank you for trying to help me today.

You do not have to keep working so hard tonight.

Imagine setting down one small piece of what you are carrying.

Not everything, just one piece.

Now ask, what helped me today even a little?

Let yourself notice one thing, take one final breath, and allow yourself to close this day with a little more kindness.
