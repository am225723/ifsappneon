Before moving into the day, pause, take one slow breath in and let it out.

Ask yourself, what quality of self-energy do I want to practice today?

Maybe calm, curiosity, compassion, clarity, courage, confidence, creativity, or connectedness.

Choose one.

Now ask, where might I need this quality today?

Let one situation come to mind.

Imagine bringing that quality with you.

Not perfectly, just enough to remember.

Silently say, today I will practice returning to this quality, one moment at a time.

Take one more breath and begin.
