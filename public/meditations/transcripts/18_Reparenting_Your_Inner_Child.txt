Welcome to Reparenting Your Inner Child.

This practice helps you offer care to a younger part from your present-day self.

Begin by settling into a comfortable position.

Take a slow breath in and let it out.

Imagine a younger part of you who may need attention.

You do not need to force an image.

You may sense an age, a feeling, a memory, or simply the presence of a younger part.

Ask gently, would it be okay if I came closer?

If the part says no, respect that.

If the part says yes, imagine approaching with warmth and patience.

Ask, what did you need back then?

Listen.

Maybe the part needed protection, comfort, belief, encouragement, stability, permission to feel, or permission to be a child.

Now ask, what do you need from me now?

Listen again.

Offer only what feels honest.

You might say, I am here now.

You matter to me.

I want to learn how to care for you.

You did not deserve to be alone with that.

We can go slowly.

Now imagine giving this child part something supportive.

A blanket.

A safe room.

A hand to hold.

A comforting phrase.

A protective boundary.

A place to play.

Let the part choose.

Now ask, how do you feel toward me right now?

Do not force trust.

If the part is unsure, that is okay.

Trust is built through repeated care.

Ask, what is one way I can show up for you this week?

Listen.

Choose one small, reparenting action.

Maybe speaking kindly to yourself, taking rest seriously, setting a boundary, making space for play, writing to the younger part, or returning to this practice.

Now, think the younger part.

Let them know you will keep learning.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out return gently to the room.
