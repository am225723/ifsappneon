Pause and bring attention to your body.

Start with your feet.

Notice contact with the floor or surface beneath you.

Now notice your legs, your hips, your back, your shoulders, your jaw, your face.

You do not need to relax anything.

Just notice.

Now ask, Where is my body asking for attention?

Bring a little breath to that place.

Ask, is there a part connected to this sensation?

If yes, simply say, I notice you.

If no, simply stay with the body.

Take one slow breath in.

And let it out.

Return to the room with a little more awareness.
