Welcome to the Parts Council Meditation.

This is a more advanced practice.

It is an invitation to listen to several parts from self-energy without forcing agreement or resolution.

If at any time this feels overwhelming, you can pause, open your eyes, return to your breath, or stop.

Begin by settling into your body.

Let yourself arrive.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Notice the room around you.

Notice that you are here in the present moment.

Now imagine a safe council space inside.

This might be a circle of chairs, a quiet room, a fire circle, a garden, a conference table, a place in nature.

Let it be a space where parts can gather without being forced.

Now invite self-energy to be present at the center of this space, not as a boss, not as a judge, but as a calm, curious, compassionate presence.

Now ask.

Which parts want to be represented in this council today?

Let parts appear one at a time.

There may be a manager, a critic, a protector, a tired part, a young part, a reactive part, a part that wants control, a part that wants escape.

Let whoever comes be noticed.

Now choose one part to speak first.

Ask, what do you want the council to know?

Listen.

Now thank that part.

Let it know it has been heard.

Now invite another part to speak.

Ask, what do you want the council to understand?

Listen.

Thank that part.

Now notice if any parts disagree with each other.

Maybe one part wants closeness and another wants distance.

One wants rest, and another wants achievement.

One wants expression, and another wants silence.

This is polarization.

It does not mean anything is wrong.

It means parts are trying to protect in different ways.

Ask the council.

What are you each trying to protect?

Listen without rushing.

Now invite self-energy to speak if it feels available.

Not to command the parts, not to fix them, but to offer presence.

You might silently say, I hear that each of you is trying to help.

I do not need you to agree immediately.

I want to understand what each of you carries.

Now ask the parts.

What do you need from self right now?

Maybe they need patience, reassurance, protection, a plan, rest, clarity, time, respect.

Imagine offering what feels possible.

Only what feels honest.

Now notice if any younger or more vulnerable part is nearby.

Do not force it to come forward.

Just notice.

If a vulnerable part is present, ask the protectors.

Would it be okay if I simply noticed this part without overwhelming it?

Respect the answer.

Now return to the whole council.

Let each part offer a word, image, feeling, or message.

Now thank the council.

Thank the parts that stayed hidden.

Thank the protectors.

Thank the vulnerable parts.

Thank the system for allowing this much contact.

Imagine closing the council respectfully.

Parts can return to their places.

The room remains available for future meetings.

Nothing has to be finished today.

Now return to your body.

Feel your breath.

Feel your hands.

Feel the surface under you.

Notice the room.

Notice the present moment.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

When you are ready, gently return.

You do not have to make every part agree today.

The council can remain open inside you.

A place where each voice can be heard, where protection can soften over time, and where self can keep learning how to lead with steadiness and care.
