Welcome to the personalized wound healing action plan.

This practice helps you choose one small healing action connected to a wound pattern you have been noticing.

You do not need to fix the whole wound today.

You are choosing one compassionate next step.

Begin by settling your body.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Now gently ask, what wound pattern has been most present for me recently?

Maybe abandonment, shame, neglect, betrayal, rejection, helplessness, or another painful theme.

Choose one pattern to focus on today.

Now ask, how does this wound show up in my daily life?

Maybe in relationships, work, avoidance, perfectionism, people-pleasing, withdrawal, self-criticism, urgency or numbing.

Notice without judgment.

Now ask,

What part of me carries this wound most strongly?

Let a part come forward if it wants to.

Ask this part.

What do you need me to understand?

Listen.

Now ask, what protector parts try to prevent this wound from being felt?

Maybe a manager part, a critic, a planner, a controller, an avoidant part, a firefighter, or a numbing part.

Thank the protectors for trying to help.

Now choose one small healing action for this week.

It should be simple.

It might be pausing before self-criticism, writing one kind sentence to a younger part, setting one small boundary, practicing one breath before reacting, noticing when a protector shows up, doing one inner system map update, or completing one life integration practice.

Now ask, what support do I need to take this action?

and what part might resist this action.

Listen.

Now create a gentle plan.

When I notice this wound pattern, I will pause and offer one small action of care.

Let the action be realistic.

Let it be kind.

Let it be small enough to do.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Thank the parts that showed up.

You are not healing everything at once.

You are building trust one step at a time.
