Welcome to Body Based Inner Child Connection.

This practice helps you notice how a younger part may communicate through body sensation.

You do not need to create a memory.

You do not need to analyze anything.

Just listen gently to the body.

Begin by finding a comfortable position.

Let your body be supported.

Take a slow breath in.

And let it out.

Now scan your body slowly.

Notice your face, your jaw, your throat, your shoulders, your chest, your stomach, your back, your hands, your legs, your feet.

Notice any place that calls your attention.

It might be tight, heavy, warm, cold, numb, tingly, restless or empty.

Just notice.

Now ask, is there a younger part connected to this body sensation?

If yes, notice gently.

If no, simply stay with the sensation as sensation.

Ask the sensation or younger part.

What do you want me to know?

Listen for words, images, emotions or a subtle sense.

Now ask, how old does this feeling seem?

Do not force an answer.

Just notice if an age, season or life chapter comes to mind.

Now ask,

What did this parrot need back then?

Maybe comfort, protection, attention, permission to feel, permission to speak, or permission to rest.

Place a hand near the body area if that feels supportive.

You do not have to touch directly.

You can simply bring warmth or attention there.

Say gently, I notice you.

I am listening.

You do not have to speak perfectly.

Your body is allowed to tell the truth slowly.

Now ask, what would help this area feel even 1% safer?

Maybe more breath, more space, a different posture, a blanket, a boundary or a word of reassurance.

Offer one small thing.

Now notice whether the sensation changes.

It may soften.

It may become stronger.

It may move.

It may stay the same.

All responses are allowed.

Now ask the younger part.

What do you want me to carry forward from this practice?

Listen.

Thank the body.

Thank the younger part.

Thank any protectors that allowed this much contact.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Return your attention to the room.

Notice what you can see.

Notice the ground or support beneath you.

When you are ready, gently return.
