Welcome to the cultivating self-energy practice.

This practice is an invitation to strengthen your connection with the steady, compassionate center of your system.

Self-energy does not need to be forced.

It is not a performance.

It is the part of you that can notice, listen, care, and respond with more spaciousness.

Begin by finding a comfortable position.

Let your body be supported.

Let your eyes close if that feels okay.

Or let your gaze rest softly.

Take a slow breath in.

And let it out.

Again, breathing in.

and breathing out.

Now notice what is present in your body.

You might notice tension, warmth, numbness, movement, restlessness, or stillness.

Whatever is here, simply notice it.

Now bring your attention to your inner world.

Ask gently.

What parts of me are active right now?

Let the answer come slowly.

You may notice a manager part, a worried part, a tired part, a protective part, a younger part, a part that wants to do this correctly, or a part that does not trust this process.

Each one is welcome to be noticed.

Now ask, how do I feel toward the parts I'm noticing?

If you feel curiosity, compassion, or calm, notice that.

If you feel judgment, fear, pressure, or impatience, notice that too.

Those reactions may be parts as well.

Nothing is wrong.

Take a moment to acknowledge any part that is trying to manage this practice.

You might say, inside, I see you.

You are trying to help.

You do not have to disappear.

Would you be willing to give me just a little space?

Now notice whether there is even a small opening.

A little space, a little breath, a little softness, a little willingness.

Let that be enough.

Now we will gently invite the qualities of self-energy.

Begin with calm.

Ask, where is calm even 1% available right now?

Maybe in your breath, maybe in your hands, maybe in the space around your body.

Now curiosity.

Ask.

Can I be interested in what is happening inside without needing to fix it immediately?

Now compassion.

Ask, can I sense that these parts are trying to help me even when their strategies are difficult?

Now clarity.

Ask, what do I understand a little more clearly about my inner world right now?

Now courage.

Ask, what is brave about being present with myself today?

Now confidence.

Ask, can I trust that I can listen slowly, one step at a time?

Now creativity.

Ask, is there another way to be with this that my system has not tried yet?

Now connectedness.

Ask, can I sense connection to my body, my breath this moment, or the parts of me that need care?

Now choose one self-quality that feels most available.

Let that quality become a gentle presence.

Not a force, not a command, just a warmth or spaciousness near you.

Now ask, which part of me most needs this quality today?

Let one part come forward if it wants to.

Notice it gently.

Where is it?

How close is it?

What does it seem to need?

Offer the self-quality to this part.

If calm is present, let calm be near it.

If compassion is present, let compassion surround it.

If curiosity is present, let curiosity listen.

If courage is present, let courage help you stay.

You might say, I am here with you.

I want to understand.

You do not have to carry this alone right now.

Notice how the part responds.

It does not have to transform.

It does not have to trust you completely.

A small shift is enough.

Now ask this part.

What do you want me to remember after this practice?

Listen.

Now thank the part for whatever it shared or did not share.

Thank your system for allowing this much contact.

Take a slow breath in and let it out.

Bring your attention back to your body.

Feel the support underneath you.

Notice the room around you.

Before you finish, name one self-energy quality you want to practice today.

When you are ready, gently return.

Carry these qualities like a lantern within you.

It does not need to shine brightly all the time.

Even a faint light can help a part know that you are here, listening and willing to lead with care.
