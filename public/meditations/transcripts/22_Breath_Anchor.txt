Pause for a moment.

Let your attention come to your breath.

You do not need to change it.

Just notice one inhale and one exhale.

Now feel where the breath is easiest to notice.

Maybe the nose, the chest, the belly, or the movement of your whole body.

Let this place become your anchor.

For the next few breaths simply return here.

Breathing in, I notice.

Breathing out, I soften.

If your mind wanders, that is okay.

Come back to the next breath.

Now ask yourself, what is one part of me that needed this pause?

Notice the answer gently.

Take one final breath and return to your day with a little more space.
