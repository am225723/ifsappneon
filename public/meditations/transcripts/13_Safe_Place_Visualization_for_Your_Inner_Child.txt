Welcome to safe place visualization for your inner child.

This practice invites you to create an inner place where a younger part of you can feel protected, welcomed, and unhurried.

You do not need to force an image.

You can sense, imagine, or simply choose the idea of a safe place.

Begin by settling into your body.

Feel the surface underneath you.

Notice the room around you.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Now imagine a place that could feel safe for a younger part of you.

It may be real or imaginary.

It may be indoors or outdoors.

It may be simple or detailed.

Maybe a cozy room, a tree house, a garden, a beach, a cabin, a soft blanket fort, a quiet library, or a sunny porch.

Let the place form slowly.

Notice the colors, the light, the temperature, the textures, the sounds.

Notice what makes this place feel safe.

Now imagine adding whatever this younger part might need.

A soft chair, a blanket, a favorite toy, a pet, a protective door, a window with warm light, art supplies, music, space to move, space to hide.

Let the place become supportive.

Now ask inside.

Is there a younger part of me that would like to visit this safe place?

If no part appears, that is okay.

The place can be prepared for later.

If a younger part appears, notice how close or far away they are.

Ask gently, would you like to come closer, stay where you are, or just look at the place from a distance?

Respect the answer.

This place does not demand anything.

Now ask the younger part.

What would help this place feel safer for you?

Maybe they want someone trustworthy nearby.

Maybe they want a locked door.

Maybe they want softness.

Maybe they want brightness.

Maybe they want quiet.

Maybe they want play.

Imagine adding what feels welcome.

Now ask, where would you like to be in this place?

Let the part choose.

They may sit, stand, hide, explore, play, or simply watch.

You do not need to direct them.

Now offer a message from self-energy.

You might say, this place is for you.

You do not have to perform here.

You do not have to explain everything.

You can come here when you need rest.

You are allowed to go slowly.

Notice how the younger part responds.

Now imagine a protective boundary around the safe place.

Only supportive energy is allowed in.

Only what this part consents to can enter.

The younger part can decide who comes close.

Now ask, is there anything you want me to remember about this place?

Now, let the younger part know that this safe place remains available.

They can return here in future practices.

They can adjust it.

They can change it.

They can make it more theirs.

Thank the younger part for any contact it allowed.

Thank your system for creating a place of care.

Now slowly return attention to your adult body.

Feel your breath.

Feel your hands.

Feel your feet.

Notice the room.

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

When you are ready, return.
