Welcome to 4-7-8 breathing.

This practice uses a longer exhale to help the body settle.

We will breathe in for four counts, hold for seven counts, and breathe out for eight counts.

If holding your breath does not feel good, you can shorten the hold or simply breathe gently.

Let this be supportive, not forced.

Begin by sitting or lying comfortably.

Let your shoulders soften.

Let your jaw loosen.

Take one natural breath in and let it out.

Now we'll begin.

Breathe in.

One, two, three, four, hold.

one two three four five six seven exhale one two three four five six seven eight again breathe in

One, two, three, four, hold.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, exhale.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight.

Let the exhale be slow and easy.

Again, inhale.

one two three four hold one two three four five six seven exhale

One, two, three, four, five, and six, seven, eight.

If you need to breathe normally at any time, do that.

Your body knows what it needs.

Again, breathe in.

One, two, three, four, hold.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, exhale.

One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight.

Now let's do a few quieter rounds.

Breathe in for four.

Hold for seven.

Exhale for eight.

Again, inhale.

Hold.

Exhale One more round.

Inhale Hold

Exhale.

Now release the counting.

Let your breath return to its natural rhythm.

Notice your body.

Notice whether anything has shifted.

Maybe there is more calm.

Maybe there is resistance.

Maybe there is no obvious change.

All of that is okay.

Take one final natural breath in and let it out.

When you are ready, return to the room.
