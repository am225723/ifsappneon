Welcome to meeting your inner child.

This practice is an invitation to gently connect with a younger part of you.

You are always allowed to go slowly.

You are allowed to stop.

You are allowed to keep your eyes open.

You do not need to force any memory, image, or feeling.

Begin by settling into a comfortable position.

Let your body know that in this moment, you are here,

Take a slow breath in and release it.

Now notice the room around you.

Notice one thing you can see, one thing you can feel, one thing you can hear.

Let this help orient you to the present moment.

Now imagine if it feels okay that there is a safe inner place

This place can be real or imagined.

It might have warm light, soft colors, a steady sound, or a sense of quiet protection.

It might be a room, a garden, a beach, a forest, a cozy chair, a place with warm light.

Let it be simple.

You are not forcing anything.

You are creating a place where a younger part could feel safe enough to be noticed.

Now ask inside.

Is there a younger part of me that would like to be known today?

If nothing comes, that is okay.

You can simply sit in this safe place and offer kindness to any younger parts who may not be ready.

If a younger part does appear, notice how close or far away they are.

Notice their age if you sense one.

Notice their expression.

Notice their posture.

Notice what they might be feeling.

You do not need to move toward them too quickly.

Ask inside.

Is it okay if I notice you?

If the answer feels like no, respect that.

You can stay at a distance.

If the answer feels like yes, imagine offering a gentle presence.

Now ask this younger part.

What do you want me to know?

Listen.

You may hear words.

You may sense feelings.

You may notice images.

You may simply feel something in your body.

Let it be enough.

Now ask, What was hard for you?

And then, what did you need that you did not receive?

If this feels tender, let yourself slow down.

You do not have to fix it all.

You are simply witnessing.

Now notice how you feel toward this younger part.

If you feel compassion, warmth, or sadness, let that be here.

If you feel numb, distant, annoyed, or unsure, notice that too.

That may be another protective part.

You can acknowledge it gently.

If it feels right, say to the younger part, I see you.

I believe that this was hard.

You deserved care.

I am here with you now.

Notice how the younger part responds.

You might ask, what would help you feel a little safer right now?

Maybe they want space, maybe closeness, maybe a blanket, maybe a kind word, maybe a place to play, maybe simply not to be rushed.

Imagine offering only what feels welcome.

Now thank this younger part for showing up in whatever way they did.

Let them know you can return slowly.

You do not have to leave them behind.

You can imagine placing them somewhere safe and comfortable inside.

Now begin to notice your adult body again, your breath, the surface under you, the room around you.

Take one slow breath in and let it out.

when you are ready, gently return.

You do not have to complete the whole story today.

Simply noticing a younger part with care is already a meaningful act of repair.

You can come back slowly with patience, warmth, and respect.
