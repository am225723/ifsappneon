Welcome to the attachment pattern reflection.

This practice helps you notice how protective parts may respond to closeness, distance, repair and connection.

This is not about labelling yourself.

It is about understanding patterns with compassion.

Begin by settling your body.

Take a slow breath in.

And let it out.

Bring to mind a recent relationship moment that felt activating.

Choose something manageable.

Not the most overwhelming moment.

Notice what happened.

Did a part want closeness?

Did a part want distance?

Did a part seek reassurance?

Did a pot shut down?

Did a pot become guarded?

Did a part feel confused, wanting connection and protection at the same time?

Now ask, what did my system believe was at stake in that moment?

Maybe being abandoned, overwhelmed, rejected, controlled, unseen, too much, or not enough.

Now notice the protective strategy.

Did you reach, withdraw, please, protest, freeze, explain, avoid, perform, become critical, or become silent?

Ask the protective part What were you trying to protect me from?

Listen

Now ask, what did a younger or more vulnerable part need in that moment?

Maybe reassurance, consistency, space, repair, honesty, gentleness or boundaries.

Ask.

What would self-energy have offered in that moment?

Maybe calm, clarity, a boundary, a repair attempt, a pause, a request, or a kind truth.

Now imagine replaying the moment with a little more self-energy present.

Not perfectly, just one small change.

What would you say?

What would you do?

What would you not do?

What would you ask for?

Now ask, what attachment pattern do I want to understand more gently rather than judge?

Take a slow breath in.

and let it out.

Thank the parts that protect you in relationships.

They learned for a reason and they can learn new options slowly with support.
